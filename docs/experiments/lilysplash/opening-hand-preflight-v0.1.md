## Audit verdict

The block is valid for opening-hand and mulligan diagnostics, but it is not yet evidence for a card
cut. The generic mulligan policy evaluates whether cheap cards are castable; it does not require
access to every commander color. It consequently kept three seven-card hands with no green source or
fixer and two hands with no blue source or fixer. That is a policy confound for a green-blue engine
deck and must be isolated from land-base challengers.

The next paired experiment should retain these exact seeds and compare two variables independently:

1. the submitted list under a commander-aware keep rule; and
2. a green-access land package under the same commander-aware keep rule.

Only after that separation should a land swap be promoted into the optimized list.
