## v2 objective

Build a materially redesigned Pauper Altar-Tron deck that preserves a recognizable artifact /
Retriever / Altar identity only where those pieces earn their slots, with competitive strength as
the primary objective.

R0 intentionally freezes the design-space search before any v2 matchup outcome exists.

Exactly three structural families may enter the first screen:

1. **COMPACT_LOOP** — reduce setup burden and dead top-end; maximize deterministic access to a
   compact recursive Altar conversion.
2. **RECURSIVE_EGGS** — raise cheap artifact velocity and graveyard recursion density so normal
   fair turns advance the combo instead of merely cycling.
3. **LEAN_TRON_HYBRID** — make Tron assembly and early mana conversion the primary clock while
   retaining only the artifact loop pieces that improve conversion after Tron.

These are architecture families, not preselected winners. Each exact candidate must be frozen
before the first comparative structural result is exposed.

## Structural boundary

An R0 challenger is not an incremental tweak. It must:

- remain Pauper legal and exactly 60/15;
- differ from v1.0 by at least 8 maindeck card slots;
- keep no card merely for project history;
- use no v1 Gate 5–11 matchup seed;
- receive no opponent-specific tuning before the first structural screen closes;
- be frozen by exact deck hash and card-count manifest before comparative results.

No fourth family may be added after results are observed.
