# Gate 7 Mono-Blue Terror policy audit v1

Status: in progress; seed-free policy admission gate.

## Question

Can the production-candidate opponent policy execute the identity-critical lines of the exact sourced
Mono-Blue Terror list well enough to admit fresh Gate 7 matchup sampling?

## Deterministic fixtures

No deck shuffles and no experimental namespace are used. The exact production profile must:

1. cast Thought Scour targeting itself when self-mill advances Tolarian Terror;
2. cast Tolarian Terror at its fully reduced colored floor;
3. cast Cryptic Serpent at its fully reduced colored floor;
4. use Counterspell on a meaningful opposing engine spell;
5. use Force Spike when a large opposing threat cannot pay the tax;
6. use Dispel to stop immediately lethal burn; and
7. deploy Murmuring Mystic before spending a same-turn Ponder when mana supports both.

Any failed fixture blocks reservation or execution of a fresh Gate 7 gameplay namespace. A full pass
clears only the opponent-policy admission requirement; it is not matchup evidence and does not
authorize a deck change or postboard work.

Frozen Industrial Waste v1.0 Control remains unchanged.
