# Ferocity Recycling — bounded research protocol v0.1

Status: initial development design; no randomized outcomes at creation. Effective research date: 2026-09-26 UTC. This is an independent project based on repository `GodaPupa/argentum-batshit-test` at `d0c78bb4cca79b7402ba65bd62b5cb621230a054`. Project branch: `ferocity-recycling/archetype-research`.

## Questions and admissible conclusions

A. Select the strongest observed Ferocity configuration within the finite search below.
B. Compare it against a competently rebuilt version without Ferocity with the same development opportunities.
C. Compare performance with a current close established benchmark, and report every gauntlet matchup.

Possible conclusions: supported new-archetype candidate; supported improvement to an existing archetype; promising but unconfirmed configuration; functional engine without demonstrated competitive advantage; no supported improvement within this search. An infrastructure failure is a suspended research checkpoint, never the fifth conclusion. No simulation result is a tournament result or a Tier-1 designation.

## Isolation and source admission

Do not modify another experiment's lists, policies, seeds, receipts or evidence. Do not use Industrial Waste's Ferocity challenger allocations or conclusions. Reusable engine components must be qualified at this project's own exact source. Follow the repository's review process for narrowly scoped engine changes. Preserve failed validation attempts. The user's explicit publication authority covers project files, code, evidence, branch and project-scoped PRs.

Archive authoritative card text, current common-printing evidence, platform/date legality and official rules links separately. Deadly Dispute is excluded by the live banned list. A verified card record does not establish release/platform legality. If Ferocity is not admitted for sanctioned Pauper, otherwise qualified simulations may run only in explicitly labeled `VERIFIED_TEXT_PRERELEASE` mode. This project does not relax any other program's release gate. Contradictory or unverified mechanics block the affected simulation.

## Candidate families — chosen before outcomes

| Family | Strategic reason | Initial Ferocity configurations | Initial no-Ferocity configurations |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| A: artifact/Shaman control (Rakdos and Grixis) | Shaman sweepers plus Familiar/fodder and a credible clock; two Rakdos slots and one Grixis slot test whether blue earns its cost | 3 | 3 |
| B: black-based Rats attrition | Repeatable board control with life management; compare mostly black with a green selection package | 3 | 3 |
| C: Orzhov Aura-recovery and evasive value | Auramancer can recover the spent Aura; white entry value and flying pressure test a distinct recovery architecture | 3 | 3 |

Nine initial Ferocity lists is the ceiling, not nine mandatory four-copy builds. Exact 60s, IDs, versions, role tags and rationales are frozen in `initial-search-manifest.json` before any randomized screen. Family C was selected by rules/resource audit, without gameplay outcomes. It may fail; no replacement fourth family is permitted.

Eligible initial packages: those explicitly named in the versioned lists and `DECK_DEVELOPMENT.md`, with mana bases fixed per list. Initial Ferocity counts: A 2/3/4, B 1/2/3, C 2/3/4; mixed Toxin configurations consume a listed candidate slot. No unlisted adaptive deck is permitted in the initial stage. At most three no-Ferocity versions per family receive the same sample as the three Ferocity versions. Remove package-dependent cards when appropriate, documenting controlled substitution versus broader rebuild. No-Ferocity cards may include Toxin Analysis, Not Dead After All and ordinary efficient removal/draw/threats; no recursion effect is free.

Mana-design checks: record total lands, untapped and tapped sources, colored activation requirements, artifact dependence and a practical sequencing policy. Do not equate source inventory with mana available by a particular turn. Crypt Rats requires black mana for X; a green splash needs a stated benefit and an actual mana budget. Artifact sacrifices reduce Familiar affinity and may remove lands.

## Evidence stages and finite budgets

| Stage | Maximum budget | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| D0 exact arithmetic | Deterministic opening-inventory probabilities for all frozen initial lists | Diagnostics only; no wins, kill turns, draws or seeds |
| D1 deterministic mechanical/pilot qualification | Fixed cases and bounded policy revisions below | Rules/policy checks, never matchup samples |
| D2 initial interactive development | 40 games/list: 8 per each of five opponents, 4 play/4 draw; 18 lists maximum = 720 games | Exploratory architecture/package selection |
| D3 one refinement round | At most 2 advancing Ferocity configurations; each incumbent plus 2 new package variants; the same incumbent-plus-2 opportunity for its best no-Ferocity comparator. 40 fresh games/list, maximum 12 lists = 480 games | One final exploratory selection only |
| E frozen preboard evaluation | Final Ferocity, final no-Ferocity, closest established benchmark; 200 games/deck/matchup, 100 play/100 draw; 3,000 games maximum | Frozen screening inference |
| C independent confirmation | Same three lists and policies, fresh trial namespace; 200 games/deck/matchup; 3,000 games maximum | Independent confirmation, no tuning |
| S sideboard development | If E passes: candidate and no-Ferocity each receive 2 sideboard variants × 5 matchups × 10 practice BO3 matches = 200 matches; sourced benchmark receives 20 matches × 5 matchups for policy development = 100; 300 matches maximum | Develop plans before confirmation; never confirmation evidence |
| P frozen postboard confirmation | If C passes and complete 60/15 plans qualified: 100 real BO3 matches/deck/matchup for three evaluated decks; 1,500 matches maximum | Match-win evidence; no conversion from game win rates |

Maximum randomized preboard games: 7,200 (720+480+3,000+3,000). Sideboard budgets count matches separately, with at most three games per match. No postboard samples precede a frozen legal 60/15 plan. No mandatory huge goldfish campaign: any goldfish debugging must consume a separately predeclared slice of D2/D3 allocations, reducing that stage's interactive budget, and cannot enter win-rate denominators.

Before D2, each candidate family and each benchmark pilot receives at most two documented revisions based on up to 24 fixed training scenarios per revision. The incumbent plus revisions remain archived. No randomized self-training outside D2/D3. Before D3, record both refinement package variants and policy changes without viewing D3 outcomes. Policies may receive at most one D3 revision before its first game; no retuning thereafter. Equal opportunity means equal budgets and tailored, qualified policies, not forcing every deck through one generic heuristic.

## Early gameplay admission

Check dependency provisioning before large-scale simulation. Use the real repository engine or another fully qualified engine with exact provenance, not a value-score substitute. Card registry presence alone is insufficient. Fixed scenarios must cover the actual candidate/comparison pool, selected disruption scenarios and relevant rules. Each action, choice, targeting step, cost payment, resolution and outcome must be supported. Unsupported choices produce an explicit fault with a preserved trace; no silent pass or guessed winner.

Required Ferocity mechanics include flash and both hybrid payments; Aura target legality and removal before resolution; attachment and continuous bonuses; the Aura's own triggered ability, distinct from a granted ability; death versus exile/bounce/replacement; return under the owner's control, tapped, with new object identity; token disappearance; loss of Aura/bonuses; cost-payment indivisibility; death/entry trigger ordering; Shaman's nonflying and friendly damage; Rats' black-only X and player damage; deathtouch/prevention/indestructible/zero; last-known information and queued abilities; actual artifact-count costs/fodder; graveyard disruption before return. Qualify Toxin lifelink and Clue costs too. See `MECHANICAL_QUALIFICATION.md` for passing and still-open items.

Each pilot can access only its own current private information, public state, and explicitly revealed information. Future library order, opponent hands, hidden exiled cards and RNG state are unavailable. Mandatory invariance tests alter hidden opponent cards and future order without altering a public decision. A complete public legal-action set must be available; declining an unsupported action is not a valid policy fallback.

Before a stage starts, an admission record must bind source commit, dependency manifest, deck/policy hashes, rules mode, completed qualification receipt, opponents, sample manifest, cap policy and trace schema. This is one concrete executable gate, not a chain of administrative placeholders.

## Gauntlet and estimand

Five equal-weight pressures, selected from the source audit: fast aggression, blue interaction, artifact resource pressure, removal attrition, and a slower engine with a real finish. Exact sources/lists live in `decks/benchmarks/` and `BENCHMARK_AUDIT.md`; prospective choices are recent Mono Red Madness, Dimir Faeries, Esper Affinity, Golgari Gardens and Monster Tron. Any source discrepancy must be resolved before opponent freeze and before D2. This five-deck sample is not an estimate of the entire Pauper metagame.

Closest established comparison for family A: source-backed Grixis artifact/Shaman list; for B: source-backed Gardens list. For C, use the strongest observed relevant established attrition benchmark from the declared pool, chosen before E and justified using D2/D3 plus strategic similarity. Benchmarks receive their own bounded pilot qualification; their sourced decklists are not modified unless a source-legality discrepancy is found and resolved before outcomes.

Primary game outcome: win=1, legal draw=0.5, loss=0. Caps, timeouts and unsupported events are unresolved attempts, not legal draws. Report win/loss/draw/unresolved separately. Per-matchup score is balanced over starting player, and aggregate score gives each matchup weight 1/5. Do not infer that a board wipe, creature return, material advantage, or successful loop constitutes winning.

## Selection, uncertainty and stopping

D2 advances at most two Ferocity configurations, ranked by equal-weight completed-game score only when all allocated attempts have admissible outcomes. Exact score ties are resolved by lexicographic candidate ID. Diagnostics inform interpretation but are not discretionary tie breakers. If invalid/unresolved attempts prevent a comparable complete sample, stop selection and repair the cause under the incident procedure; do not silently drop the problematic matchup. A family may be rejected for a proven rules/legality or deck-integrity defect; it is not rejected merely for weak opponent-free damage output.

D3 selects one final Ferocity list by the same score/tiebreak rule. Select the best-developed compatible no-Ferocity list, without leaving unnecessary package baggage. Record whether these are a tight substitution or a broader build comparison. D3 does not run if fewer than one valid configuration remains. After completing D3, stop at an exploratory no-improvement finding rather than spending the independent evaluation budget when this exact criterion holds: no surviving Ferocity score exceeds its best family baseline and no single matchup gain is at least 10 percentage points. This is a development conclusion, not an independent proof of no effect.

E and C require no missing or unresolved game outcomes for performance acceptance. Use ten fixed strata (five matchups × two starting seats), equal weight 1/10. Per-stratum score variance is the unbiased sample variance of win=1/draw=0.5/loss=0. For an aggregate mean, variance is the sum of (1/10)^2 × s_h^2/n_h. For independent-deck differences, add the two mean variances. The primary two-sided 95% interval is point estimate ±1.959963984540054 standard errors, clipped only for display to the feasible score/difference range. Per-matchup calculations use two equal-weight seat strata. Also report Wilson binary-win intervals (legal draws excluded from this explicitly labeled binary-win calculation). Small-sample development comparisons are descriptive. Before E, freeze an analysis implementation and validate it with deterministic artificial data; publish no performance acceptance based solely on the normal approximation. Sensitivity analysis uses 20,000 bootstrap resamples independently within each deck/matchup/seat stratum, percentile 2.5%/97.5% bounds, with a recorded analysis-only RNG seed frozen before E. If primary and sensitivity classifications disagree, the finding is inconclusive. No shared numeric game seed is described as a valid pair.

Meaningful Ferocity improvement threshold: +5 percentage points in the equal-weight aggregate. Advancement E→C requires observed Ferocity improvement >=5 points, its two-sided 95% difference interval excludes zero, aggregate competitiveness versus established benchmark no worse than -5 points in point estimate, and no important matchup (fast aggression or blue interaction) more than 10 points worse in point estimate. These are screening thresholds; uncertainty can still preclude confirmation.

Supported confirmation requires fresh C outcomes: Ferocity improvement >=5 points with 95% lower confidence limit >0, aggregate noninferiority to the established benchmark with 95% lower limit above -5 points, and per-matchup 95% lower difference bounds above -10 points against BOTH the no-Ferocity comparator and the established benchmark in fast aggression and blue interaction. An interval crossing a harm margin is inconclusive. A positive lower bound plus an observed >=5-point gain supports a positive benefit whose point estimate is meaningful; it does not prove the true benefit exceeds 5 points. Only a lower difference bound >=5 points supports that stronger magnitude claim. Failure/inconclusiveness reaches the finite stopping rule; do not expand sample size or tune lists. Narrow matchup benefits are reported separately without promotion to overall advantage. E and C are analyzed separately first; pooled estimates are secondary and labeled.

The sourced benchmark and all gauntlet decks retain their exact published 75s during S; only their boarding/gameplay policies develop. Candidate and no-Ferocity sideboards may develop within the two-variant budget; their main 60s stay frozen. Candidate sideboards and all plans must freeze before C is exposed, so C cannot train postboard decisions. P reports actual BO3 match wins/draws/losses per matchup and aggregate. Sideboard/game policies and play/draw after game losses are frozen for both sides. Do not call P confirmed superiority from preboard game scores. If resources cannot execute the specified precision, stop as incomplete/inconclusive and publish the actual accepted scope; do not retroactively label a small sample sufficient.

## Seeds, raw data and replay

Namespace: `FEROCITY_RECYCLING/v0.1/<stage>/<deck-id>/<opponent-id>/<seat>/<index>`. No seed values exist at protocol creation. A stage's allocation identities and randomized seed generation procedure must be frozen before its first draw. Use independently generated per-game streams by default. Store a private seed commitment before evaluation; publish completed seeds/traces only when future allocations cannot be inferred. No future evaluation material is published in an earlier development checkpoint.

Every attempt has an exclusive durable claim and immutable identity. Record source/dependencies, deck/policy hashes, seed/RNG identity, full authoritative action/event trace, masked decision observations, starting player, result or failure, and cap. Replay must reproduce the event/action digest at the same source. Replay is debugging evidence, never a new trial. An outcome exposure counter increments when an outcome is first inspected; no rerolling unfavorable outcomes.

Default runtime cap is 6,000 submitted actions or 150 complete turns per game; wall-time cap 300 seconds excluding external CI scheduling. Caps are unresolved technical outcomes. Freeze actual cap implementation and fixtures before D2; changes are result-affecting protocol revisions, not invisible retries.

## Diagnostics

Record Ferocity drawn/cast/stranded/redundant; role (sweeper, protection, combat, value); resources spent; friendly/opposing deaths; successful returns and subsequent useful actions; actual resolved entry/death triggers; life pressure; Aura/target removal; graveyard disruption; recovery timing; engine-without-finish; postboard hate. Direct measurements and causal interpretations remain separate. Presence in a winning game is not a causal attribution. Do not count preserving a creature twice or ignore the card/mana cost of the protection effect. Counterfactual language needs a predeclared valid replay comparison.

## Incidents, publication and conclusion

On a result-affecting bug, preserve all original attempts, scope every affected stage/source, invalidate affected performance use explicitly, and document the fix and regression. A frozen stage cannot acquire extra favorable replacements: affected attempts retain their IDs and an amended, prospectively committed repair protocol is required; maximum attempted allocation count and replacement cap must be stated before replacement execution. If valid repair cannot fit the finite budget, stop inconclusive. Unrelated project inputs are never changed to pass a gate.

Before publication review the diff for credentials, personal/unrelated files and future concealed evaluations. Publish reviewed project work to the isolated branch and a scoped PR. Keep `CURRENT_STATUS.md` and `RESEARCH_REPORT.md` accurate; write `FINAL_CONCLUSION.md` only on an actual stopping rule. Final performance deliverables require a supported 60/15, import list, practical primer/mulligans/boarding plans and explicit account of what Ferocity adds or fails to add. Prototype lists may be published sooner, clearly labeled unqualified.
