# Ferocity Recycling — Bounded Archetype Discovery Protocol

Frozen before randomized outcomes: 2026-09-25.

## Questions

A. Strongest Ferocity configuration found within this bounded search.
B. Whether Ferocity improves that shell versus a competently rebuilt no-Ferocity version.
C. Whether the resulting deck is competitive against relevant established archetypes under the tested conditions.

These are separate gates.

## Initial families

1. **Rakdos artifact-sacrifice control.** Ferocity is tested as a flash deathtouch/return layer around creature sweepers, artifact fodder, value creatures, sacrifice draw, and a real clock. Krark-Clan Shaman lines must charge friendly casualties.
2. **Black / Golgari Rats attrition.** Crypt Rats is the primary sweeper hypothesis. Green survives only if permanent selection/recovery or threats justify its source cost. Mostly-black variants are allowed.
3. **Orzhov Aura recovery/value.** Selected before outcomes. Tests whether white value/evasive creatures plus Aura recovery make Ferocity's spent-card cost recoverable without producing an unacceptably slow engine.

No fourth family may be added during initial development.

## Search budget

Maximum nine complete Ferocity configurations:
- Rakdos: 4
- Rats: 3
- Orzhov: 2

Ferocity count may be 2–4. Mixed Ferocity/Toxin Analysis packages are eligible. Package changes may span multiple slots and must be versioned as packages.

Each Ferocity configuration receives a competent no-Ferocity comparator developed under the same diagnostic budget. Removing Ferocity also removes stranded support whose only purpose was Ferocity.

## Development

Before interactive random samples, every admitted identity and required mechanic must pass deterministic qualification. Functional screens may measure colored-source access, opening hands, fodder, stranded support, threat density, and resource costs; they are diagnostic only.

Initial randomized development budget: at most 20 preboard games per configuration/comparator pair against each development opponent admitted for that family. Development data may select at most two Ferocity configurations.

One refinement round is permitted for those survivors: at most two revised complete configurations per survivor and at most 30 development games per revised configuration across the declared development opponents. Selection rule: advance the configuration with the best predeclared aggregate primary outcome subject to no material rules/policy validity failure and no catastrophic pressure-cell regression.

Final evaluation and confirmation data may not be used for retuning.

## Final evaluation contract gate

Before evaluation outcomes, freeze exact 60/15s, opponent 75s, pilots, play/draw allocation, seed namespace, sample size, matchup weights, meaningful-improvement threshold, uncertainty method, acceptable matchup losses, and stopping rule.

Target pressure classes: fast aggression; blue tempo/counters; artifact resource pressure; removal-heavy attrition; combo/slow engine that punishes a weak clock. Contemporary candidates identified for source verification include Madness, Faeries, Affinity, Gardens, and Tron; names are not final admission until exact source lists are archived.

## Evidence separation

Deterministic fixtures, exploratory development, frozen evaluation, independent confirmation, and postboard matches are separate ledgers. Failed runs are preserved. No rerolls. Identical numeric seeds alone do not establish a paired comparison.

## Stopping

Stop at the predeclared budget and report one of: supported new-archetype candidate; supported improvement to an existing archetype; promising but unconfirmed; functional engine without competitive advantage; or no supported Ferocity improvement within the bounded search.
