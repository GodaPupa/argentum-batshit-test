# Rules and Legality Audit

Retrieval date: 2026-09-25.

## Ferocity of the Hunt

Research text recorded from current Reality Fracture card records:

- Mana cost: {1}{B/G}
- Enchantment — Aura
- Common
- Flash
- Enchant creature
- Enchanted creature gets +1/+0 and has deathtouch.
- When enchanted creature dies, return that card to the battlefield tapped under its owner's control.

Reality Fracture is reported for release on 2026-10-02. Therefore this project treats Ferocity gameplay before release as **verified-text prerelease simulation**, not sanctioned Pauper evidence. Current third-party legality displays conflict because the card is unreleased; release-date admission is fail-closed.

## Pauper ban correction

Deadly Dispute is excluded. Wizards' live Banned & Restricted page lists Deadly Dispute among Pauper banned cards. Wizards' March 31, 2025 announcement records the ban and its rationale.

## Mechanical implications admitted for qualification

The return trigger is on the Aura and triggers when the enchanted creature dies. The returned object enters tapped under its owner's control and is a new object. Ferocity itself is not returned by that trigger. Exile, bounce, or a replacement that prevents the creature from dying does not satisfy the dies trigger. A departed token cannot be returned. Target removal before the Aura resolves can make the Aura spell fail to resolve for lack of a legal target.

No repeatable loop is credited unless every component, mana payment, card movement, priority window, and reset condition is explicitly restored and recorded.

## Admission rule

Prerelease deterministic fixtures and clearly labeled prerelease development may run against verified text. Any claim about live Pauper legality or sanctioned tournament applicability requires a fresh post-release legality check.
