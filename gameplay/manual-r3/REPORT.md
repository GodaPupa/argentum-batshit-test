## Lane A: Encore

The exact rejected action is submission 3562/global turn 78 of seed 202610190801, designated candidate Seat 4. It was generated by CURRENT Seat 3 (`manual-mirror-2`), not by the r2 candidate. Attacker `e756` (Amphin Mutineer token) must attack `manual-mirror-1`; `e757` (another token) must attack `manual-mirror-3`. The AI sent both tokens and ordinary attacker `e232` (Bane of Progress) to `manual-mirror-1`. The engine returned `Amphin Mutineer must attack its designated defender this turn if able`, emitted no events, and left 3,561 accepted actions with no winner.

`Strategist`'s combat fast path delegates to `CombatAdvisor` and `CombatSeed`. The seed chooses one low-life victim and assigns all required attackers there; it loses the per-token defender requirement. An illegal seed can survive the local-search evaluation fallback. The Encore component and rules rejection are correct. Official Encore guidance requires each token to attack its associated opponent if able: [Commander Legends mechanics](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/commander-legends-mechanics-2020-10-26).

The separate `manual-encore-defenders-r3` profile sets a new `respectRequiredAttackDefenders` option, default false. The seed preserves engine-enumerated mandatory attackers' designated defenders and bypasses its single-defender lethal shortcut when the assignments split. Local search cannot remove mandatory attackers, preserving the assignment. This is an AI selection correction, not a rules relaxation. CURRENT/r2 leave the flag false.

Tests replay the archived prefix and rejection, assert CURRENT's original rejected choice, submit the candidate choice to the real engine, cover another legal ordinary attacker, use an explicitly labelled controlled four-living-player variant, and cover an impossible required defender. The original archived checkpoint itself has one eliminated player; its smoke is an authentic continuation of that four-seat game, not a fresh four-living-player start.

A remaining strategy limitation is that the existing combat simulator evaluates blocking through one defending player. This repair establishes valid required assignments in the reproduced case; it does not establish optimal multi-defender combat search or all combinations of competing attack requirements.
