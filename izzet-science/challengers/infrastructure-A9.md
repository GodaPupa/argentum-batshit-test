# Experimental notes
- 36 lands is the v0.1 control.
- 10 dedicated mana-infrastructure slots are the v0.1 control.
- Lava Spike + Desperate Ritual is the primary deterministic damage loop with Izzet Guildmage.
- Dramatic Reversal is the secondary mana engine; Capsize / Rolling Thunder / Kaervek's Torch are candidate conversion outlets.
- High Tide / Snap / Hidden Strings are retained as the experimental untap module.
- Archaeomancer / Mnemonic Wall / Izzet Chronarch are utility recursion only in v0.1. No Ghostly Flicker / Displace / Peregrine Drake package is included.
- This is a construction freeze candidate, not accepted performance evidence. Card legality and engine behavior must pass validation before gameplay results are accepted.
