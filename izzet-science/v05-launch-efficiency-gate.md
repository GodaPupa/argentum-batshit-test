# v0.5 Gate — Launch Efficiency

Parent: Izzet Science?! v0.4
Accepted control clock: 6.302% cumulative deterministic lethal by T10; earliest T5.

Why pivot:
v0.4 improved pair acquisition only +0.120 pp by T10. At T10, pair+commander-ready remains ~9.638% while lethal state is ~6.292%, leaving a large launch-resource gap. The next experiment targets that gap rather than adding another marginal selection spell.

Hypothesis classes:
A. Cost reduction that meaningfully lowers the pre-refund launch burden for the Spike + spliced Ritual + first Guildmage-copy sequence.
B. Red-producing acceleration that is useful outside the combo and does not recreate four-mana-rock clunk.
C. Untap/ritual effects already useful as normal spells that increase the probability of paying the initial six-mana sequence.

Constraints:
- Candidate must have a common printing and be legal for Izzet Guildmage PDH.
- Must have standalone utility; no dead-card combo-only package.
- Exact Oracle interaction with splice and Guildmage activation costs must be verified before modeling.
- Do not assume generic spell-cost reducers reduce Guildmage activated abilities.
- Do not assume a reducer changes splice costs unless rules/text support it.
- Do not change more than one slot per initial challenger.

Primary metrics:
- pair+commander-ready to lethal conversion
- cumulative lethal T5-T10
- first-lethal increments
- ordinary U/R/UU action execution
- commander deployment
- Reversal/High Tide collateral effects
