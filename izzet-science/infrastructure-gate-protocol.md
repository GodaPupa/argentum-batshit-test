Parent baseline: Izzet Science?! v0.2
v0.2 commit: ee6ab120300816be8828f65684d7bd97f62dacac
Mana base: 19 Island / 7 Mountain.

Question:
Do all 10 dedicated mana-infrastructure cards earn their slots, or can the deck reduce clunky infrastructure while preserving meaningful Guildmage/control development?

Frozen v0.2 infrastructure:
Everflowing Chalice
Fellwar Stone
Mind Stone
Star Compass
Sky Diamond
Fire Diamond
Izzet Signet
Network Terminal
Ur-Golem's Eye
Sisay's Ring

Initial candidate classes:
A — 9 infrastructure: remove one four-mana colorless rock.
B — 8 infrastructure: remove both four-mana colorless rocks.
C — retain 10 but replace clunkiest source(s) with lower-cost/flexible mana if a legal common candidate is justified.

Primary metrics:
- actual-hand U/R/UU execution
- Guildmage actionable and hold-up states
- Reversal neutral/positive thresholds
- infrastructure deployed by T3/T4/T5/T6
- opening infrastructure burden
- residual mana after development

Design rule:
A cut rock slot is NOT automatically converted into another land. Replacement spell identity must be tested separately; first isolate infrastructure-count effects using declared neutral placeholders/exclusion analysis where possible.

First target:
Sisay's Ring and Ur-Golem's Eye are the highest-priority challenge because each costs 4 and produces only colorless, making them slow for Guildmage color requirements despite strong Reversal gross output.

No v0.2 cards are changed until a challenger is explicitly materialized and sampled.
