## Purpose

Exercise the complete paired Capsize-interaction path at modest cost before any
official interactive pilot receives a seed. Two earlier pilots showed why fixed
fixtures and schema tests alone are not enough to establish reachability safety.

## Qualification boundary

- 1,024 matched pairs / 2,048 complete T1–T10 trajectories;
- accepted v0.7 deck in both arms;
- accepted Phase-14 Capsize tutor policy only in the policy arm;
- fixed coordinate `1`, already public in paired-RNG regression vectors and not an
  experimental seed;
- Phase-10 shared exact payment engine and Phase-15 interaction semantics;
- Phase-16 fixed-event aggregation and strict summary validation;
- all three exposed experimental runners required to remain retired;
- no summary serialization, outcome-field printing, effect estimate, artifact
  payload containing outcomes, or performance interpretation.

The sweep may construct and validate the full paired summary in memory. Its durable
output is restricted to identity, execution counts, zero-outcome accounting, and
the pass/fail disposition. A failure invalidates the qualification and authorizes
neither repair-by-rerun nor selective outcome inspection.

## Authorization

Success proves only that a nontrivial set of complete paired trajectories traverses
generation, isolated RNG, tutor execution, interaction telemetry, aggregation, and
all frozen invariants without an exception. It is not admissible performance
evidence and cannot support promotion or rejection.

This gate assigns and consumes zero experimental seeds. It authorizes no official
pilot, card change, opponent model, tempo claim, survival claim, or win-rate claim.
The accepted Phase-14 behavior policy and exact v0.7 card control remain unchanged.
