# v0.9 Position 1 — Engine Gap Triage Correction A

Disposition: `V09_POSITION1_ENGINE_GAP_TRIAGE_CORRECTION_A`

The accepted triage classified Boreal Druid, Deepwood Denizen, and Owlbear as
`registry_only`. Batch-A review showed that classification was too broad.

Correction:
- Boreal Druid remains `registry_only`: its relevant printed behavior is a tap
  mana ability producing one colorless mana.
- Deepwood Denizen moves to `reusable_mechanic`: vigilance plus an activated
  draw ability whose generic activation cost is dynamically reduced by +1/+1
  counters on creatures controlled.
- Owlbear moves to `reusable_mechanic`: trample plus an enters-the-battlefield
  draw trigger.

No deck identity, seed, assignment, outcome, or official-game state changes.

Batch A is therefore narrowed to Boreal Druid only. Deepwood Denizen and Owlbear
return to later reusable-mechanic implementation batches.
