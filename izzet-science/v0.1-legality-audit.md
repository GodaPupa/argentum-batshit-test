## Governing PDH rule set
PDH Home Base multiplayer rules:
- Commander: uncommon creature/vehicle/spacecraft.
- 99: commons.
- Paper and MTGO rarity releases count; Arena is excluded.
- PDH-specific banned list: Mystic Remora, Rhystic Study, ante cards, Conspiracy card type, and cards removed by Wizards from all constructed formats.

Important: the 60-card Pauper banned list is NOT imported wholesale into multiplayer PDH. Therefore cards such as High Tide, Frantic Search, and Treasure Cruise are not disqualified merely because they are banned in 60-card Pauper.
