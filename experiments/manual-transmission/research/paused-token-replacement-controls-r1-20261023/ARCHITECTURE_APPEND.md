
### Pending token-copy instruction preparation

Fresh source/target token-copy instructions reserve deterministic ids and retain future copiable cards, choices, Aura hosts and defenders in a serializable plan. These future objects are not in the entity map or any zone while entry choices are outstanding. The required original entry snapshot excludes executable continuation frames. Each continuation retains the complete EffectContext and all targets.

After every choice completes, existing token constructors commit the prepared choices/cards, entry history, counters and delayed riders. The complete instruction freezes entry snapshots and trigger observations before an enclosing continuation advances. Creation-time token abilities and public face-down characteristics use the shared copiable-value helpers. Separate Composite instructions remain distinct. A printed mode choice and each granted riot instance keep independent data; granted modes do not overwrite the printed mode.

Legacy already-published token-tail and Aura continuations remain registered with their original schemas. This change does not rewind legacy partial arrivals. The normal legacy fixtures must still pass byte-for-byte. Full replacement ordering, token-from-definition/other producers, paused ForEach spanning different copy bodies, intrinsic-counter boundary completeness and complete UI/gameplay admission remain separate gates.
