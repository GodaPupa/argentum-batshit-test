
### Entry counter modifier source boundary

Entry counter placement carries the pre-entry source state through own and granted enters-with replacements. Additive and doubling replacement providers come from that boundary, plus the prepared entering object itself; its own applicable replacement remains visible while a resolving permanent is still on the stack. Other simultaneous entrants cannot become replacement providers merely because an implementation loop inserted them first. Intrinsic cast-entry counters receive the same source boundary. Ordinary non-entry counter placement retains its current-state scan.

This change freezes replacement-source existence. Recipient filters retain the prepared-state evaluation used by the existing helper; complete hypothetical filter projection, competing replacement ordering, and every legacy token/land/Aura producer are separate gates. Callers that do not supply a pre-entry boundary still default to their current state. These limitations must not be promoted to complete entry or competitive admission.
