
### Entry counter modifier source boundary

Entry counter placement carries the pre-entry source state through own and granted enters-with replacements. Additive and doubling providers come from that boundary. The prepared entering subject supplies only replacements that exclusively affect itself, represented by RecipientFilter.Self; its general subset replacements do not apply to its own entry under CR614.12. This remains true while the subject is on the stack. Other simultaneous entrants cannot become replacement providers because an implementation loop inserted them first. Intrinsic cast-entry counters receive the same boundary. Ordinary non-entry counter placement retains its current-state scan.

This change addresses source existence and the explicit self-exclusive recipient. Arbitrary filter equivalence, complete hypothetical filter projection, competing replacement ordering, event-wide counter aggregation and every legacy token/land/Aura producer remain separate gates. Callers that do not supply a pre-entry boundary still default to their current state. These limitations cannot be promoted to complete entry or competitive admission.
