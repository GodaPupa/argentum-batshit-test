## E2 hypothesis

Maindeck: unchanged.

Sideboard delta:
-1 Weather the Storm
-1 Annul
+2 Lightning Bolt

Rationale:
- The opponent's highest-leverage acceleration/payoff creatures include Priest of Titania and Timberwatch Elf.
- Current matchup guidance emphasizes denying Priest and surviving turns 1-4 rather than spending removal indiscriminately on every one-mana elf.
- Lightning Bolt gives Face Value cheap point interaction that can remove Priest/Timberwatch before the Elves engine snowballs while preserving Breath Weapon as the broad reset.
- This is materially distinct from E1: E1 increased one-damage sweep density; E2 adds precise early removal.
